```
**Watchlist:** [TICKER1, TICKER2, ...]

### [TICKER]
- **Catalyst:** [News, earnings, technical, etc.]
- **Pre-market move:** +/-X%
- **Key levels:** Support $X / Resistance $X
- **Volume:** Xk pre-market vs Xk avg
- **Setup quality:** Strong / Moderate / Weak
- **Action:** Watching / Skipping / Trading
- **Notes:** [Anything notable]
```
